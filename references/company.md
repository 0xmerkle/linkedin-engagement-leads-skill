# What we sell

## Instantly.ai

A cold-email outreach platform. Four things in one product:

- **Sending infrastructure** — inboxes and domains you send from, kept separate from your main domain so
  volume can never damage its deliverability
- **Warmup and inbox rotation** — automated warming, and spreading volume across many inboxes
- **Campaign sequencing** — multi-step sequences, A/B testing, reply detection
- **A B2B lead database** — contact data you can pull campaigns from directly

## Pricing

Self-serve, roughly **$37 to $358 a month** depending on sending volume and database access. No sales
process for most customers — they sign up and start sending.

## What we actually compete on

Deliverability. Anyone can send email; landing in the inbox at volume is the hard part, and it is what
customers churn over. When someone talks about reply rates falling, burned domains, spam folders or
warming inboxes, they are describing our category whether or not they name a tool.

## Where we fit in a stack

Upstream of us: wherever the list comes from — a scraper, an enrichment waterfall, a CRM export. We do
not find people; we email them.

Downstream of us: the CRM, and whoever handles the replies.

This matters when scoring. Someone complaining about *finding* leads is adjacent, not core. Someone
complaining about *reaching* them is squarely ours.
