# Who we sell to

This file decides every score. Apply it strictly: if a person does not clearly match one of the segments
below, they are not a buyer, however good their title sounds.

## Best customers, in order

1. **Lead generation, outbound and B2B marketing agencies** running cold email for clients. Our single
   best segment: they send at volume, they feel deliverability pain daily, and they buy fast. An agency
   owner or founder is a strong buyer.
2. **Founders and sales leaders at B2B SaaS companies, roughly 5–200 people**, running their own
   outbound. Titles: Founder, CEO, CRO, VP Sales, Head of Growth, Head of Demand Gen.
3. **SDR and BDR teams** sending cold email at volume. Titles: SDR Manager, Head of Sales Development,
   Outbound Lead.
4. **GTM engineers and RevOps people** who own the outbound system. They often influence rather than sign,
   but they choose the tooling.

## Strong buying signals

- Talking about deliverability, domain reputation, spam folders, inbox warming or reply rates
- Describing outbound run across several stitched-together tools
- Complaining about manual steps between finding a lead and contacting them
- Mentioning volume — thousands of sends, multiple inboxes, several client campaigns

## Disqualifiers — score low

- No outbound sales motion at all
- Enterprise procurement roles, or companies large enough to need a formal sales process
- Students, job seekers, and recruiters hiring for their own company
- **Founders of stealth or pre-product companies with no customers.** They cannot buy anything, however
  well the title matches. This is the single most common way a list gets padded with false positives.
- Anyone whose work is unrelated to sales or marketing

## Competitors — score 0

Companies that **sell** cold email sending, warmup, deliverability or sequencing software:

Smartlead, Lemlist, Woodpecker, Mailshake, Reply.io, Saleshandy, Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft, Clay,
Snov.io, Hunter, Mailreef, Piperlink.

## The line that matters most

**Sells the software → competitor. Uses it for clients → customer.**

An agency running campaigns on Smartlead is a prospect we should be trying to win, not a competitor. A
company building a Smartlead alternative is a competitor no matter how good their title looks.

When in doubt, ask what the company charges its own customers for. If the answer is software that sends or
sequences email, they compete with us. If the answer is campaigns, strategy or headcount, they buy from us.
